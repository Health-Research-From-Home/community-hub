Patient and Public Involvement and Engagement (PPIE) has been embedded across the Health Research from Home programme, and is led jointly by patient partner Lynn Laidlaw, and academic rheumatologist Charlotte Sharp. We collaborated with Vocal who supported us to deliver our PPIE strategy. We hope you will find the resources that we have developed with our patient and public contributors useful when considering how and when you might work with patients and the public in smartphones and wearables research.
