Predicting pain improvement with a digital health app

August, 2026

This month’s edition of Tap into Research looks at whether data collected through a smartphone app can help identify which people with inflammatory arthritis are most likely to experience short-term pain relief.

Researchers analysed data from 914 adults using the Mida Rheuma App, a digital self-management tool for tracking symptoms and supporting lifestyle changes. They found that one in four people experienced a meaningful reduction in pain after 12 weeks. Higher baseline pain increased the likelihood of improvement, while higher fatigue reduced it. Better sleep quality also improved the chances of pain relief, and people who completed the app’s daily tasks more regularly tended to do better.

The findings suggest that real-world data from digital tools could help shape more personalised guidance for managing pain, particularly by focusing on fatigue and sleep.
