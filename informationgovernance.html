Robust information governance in S&W studies is essential because:

  • Highly sensitive, personal data can be collected from S&Ws – need to protect participants and public trust
  • Data flows can be complex
  • Data linkage increases risk – supports secure flows and linkage
  • Legal compliance is mandatory
  • Underpins ethics approvals
